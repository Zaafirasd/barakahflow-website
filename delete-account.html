Delete your BarakahFlow account

You can delete your account in the app from Profile → Delete Account. If you cannot sign in or have already uninstalled the app, use the email request below. We verify ownership before deleting an account.

Email Account Deletion Request
Or send an email directly to: barakahflowapp@gmail.com
Please send your request from the exact email address associated with your BarakahFlow account so we can verify ownership before actioning it.

What gets deleted

Deletion removes your account and linked records from the active service, including:

  1. Account profile, name and email
  2. Linked accounts and balances
  3. Logged transactions and categories
  4. Budgets, bills and savings goals
  5. Optional asset records, saved calculations and related settings
  6. Push notification device tokens

Data held by our service providers

Our providers support hosting, storage, sign-in and notifications. Account deletion removes the linked records from the active service. Provider logs and any backup copies may remain until their retention periods expire. See our Privacy Policy for more about how information is handled.

How long it takes

Requests are actioned within 30 days of verification, and a confirmation is sent once complete.

What may remain

Limited support or security records are kept only as long as needed for legal obligations, security or resolving disputes. Account deletion does not remove files you have exported or saved yourself. If you used the app on other devices, you can remove their local copies through the app's data controls or browser settings.
