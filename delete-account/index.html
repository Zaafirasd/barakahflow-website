Delete your BarakahFlow account

This page is for requesting deletion of a BarakahFlow account and all data associated with it. If you still have the BarakahFlow app installed on your device, the fastest route is Settings → Delete Account inside the app, where deletion is immediate. This page exists for people who have already uninstalled the app or cannot sign in.

Email Account Deletion Request
Or send an email directly to: barakahflowapp@gmail.com
Please send your request from the exact email address associated with your BarakahFlow account so we can verify ownership before actioning it.

What gets deleted

Requesting deletion permanently removes your account and everything linked to it:

  1. Account profile, name and email
  2. Linked accounts and balances
  3. Logged transactions and categories
  4. Budgets, bills and savings goals
  5. Gold and silver holdings
  6. Investment records
  7. Zakat calculation method, hawl dates and Zakat history
  8. Push notification device tokens

Data held by our service providers

BarakahFlow’s data is hosted with third-party infrastructure providers. When an account is deleted, the data is removed from BarakahFlow’s own systems and deletion is requested from those providers in line with their retention schedules:

  • Supabase: Our database and authentication provider. Your account, profile records, and all financial data are purged from Supabase servers upon deletion.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): Delivers push notifications to Android devices. Device tokens are purged from our records upon deletion.
  • Web Push: Delivers push notifications in browsers and on iPhone. Device tokens and push endpoints are purged from our records upon deletion.
  • Vercel: Our hosting provider. Ephemeral server access and runtime logs roll off in accordance with Vercel’s standard log retention schedules.

How long it takes

Requests are actioned within 30 days of verification, and a confirmation is sent once complete.

What we keep

Nothing. BarakahFlow retains no personal financial data, account credentials, transaction logs, or profile records once an account is deleted.
