A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.

Something else on your mind? Get in touch.

Is BarakahFlow free?+

Yes. BarakahFlow is free to use, with no subscription and no ads.

Is a Barakah Card a payment card?+

No. It is a visual part of your financial dashboard and a way to recognise your check-ins. It is not a bank account, payment card, credit product or financial reward.

Do I need to connect my bank?+

No. Add your accounts and balances yourself, then record income and expenses manually. BarakahFlow does not automatically import bank transactions.

How do I unlock the card editions?+

Essential is your starting card. One accepted first check-in unlocks Emerald. Three accepted weekly check-ins after your first unlock Midnight Gold. Eight accepted weekly check-ins in total and two complete financial-period reviews unlock Pearl Platinum. Weekly checks are at least seven days apart. There is no streak to maintain and earned editions stay yours.

Where can I use BarakahFlow?+

Open BarakahFlow in your browser or download the Android APK. On iPhone, open the web app in Safari and use Share, then Add to Home Screen.

How is my data handled?+

Your account data is protected with per-user access controls. BarakahFlow does not sell your data or show ads. The website uses technical analytics and anonymous visit and launch counters. Read the privacy policy for data storage, administrative access and deletion details.
