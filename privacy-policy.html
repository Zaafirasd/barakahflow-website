YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how we handle information when you use the BarakahFlow app and website. We do not sell your personal data or use your financial records for advertising.

1. Who we are

BarakahFlow is a personal finance service operated by the BarakahFlow team in the United Arab Emirates. We are responsible for how your information is used in the service. You can contact us at barakahflowapp@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

  • Account and preferences. Your email, profile details, sign-in information, currencies, time zone and settings. If you use Google sign-in, we receive the basic profile information it shares with us.
  • Records you add. Account balances, transactions, budgets, bills, savings goals, and any optional asset details, calculation inputs and method preferences you choose to save.
  • Reminders. Notification preferences and the device or browser registration needed to deliver reminders you enable.
  • Technical and usage information. Network information such as your IP address, browser and device details, pages visited, referrers, approximate country, and information about errors and performance. We also count actions such as visits, app launches and completed setup.
  • Messages to us. Your contact details and the information you include when you ask for help or make a request.

3. How we use it

We use this information to manage your account, save and sync your records, provide calculations and features you use, deliver enabled reminders, respond to requests, and keep the service reliable and secure. Technical information and usage counts help us understand what is working and improve the service.

We process information to provide the service you request, protect and maintain it, and meet legal obligations. Where consent is required, we ask for it. You can withdraw that consent; this does not affect processing that was lawful before you withdrew it.

4. Service providers and access

We use providers for hosting, account access, data storage, security, analytics and notifications. They process the information needed to perform those services. Our core providers include Supabase for account access and storage, Vercel for hosting and website analytics, and Google for optional sign-in and Android notification delivery. Browser notifications use your browser's push service.

Authorised team members and providers may access information when needed to operate, secure or support the service. We may also disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users and the service. We do not share your financial records with advertisers.

Our providers operate in more than one country, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live. International transfers are subject to the protections required by applicable law.

5. Cookies, local storage and analytics

The app uses cookies and device storage for sign-in, preferences and saved or offline records. Local copies can include financial information. You can remove local data through the app's data controls or your browser settings. Clearing device storage does not delete your online account.

The website uses Vercel Web Analytics and aggregate usage counters. Vercel Web Analytics does not use third-party cookies; it measures visits using information from web requests. We do not send the contents of your financial records as analytics events.

If you enable the website's optional phone-tilt effect, motion readings are processed on your device and are not stored or sent to us.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep account information and records while your account is active. You can delete your account from Profile → Delete Account, or use our account deletion page if you cannot sign in. We may need to verify ownership before acting on a request.

Deletion removes your account and linked financial records from the active service. Limited support or security records are kept only as long as needed for legal obligations, security or resolving disputes. Provider logs and any backup copies may remain until their retention periods expire. Files you have exported or saved yourself remain under your control.

7. Your choices and rights

You can update records and preferences in the app, disable optional notifications, and delete your account. You can also contact us to request access to, a copy of, or correction of your personal data.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request erasure, restrict or object to processing, transfer your data, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. We will consider requests under the applicable law and may ask for information to verify your identity.

8. Protecting your information

We use encrypted connections, access controls and other safeguards to protect information. No online service or device storage can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your sign-in details private, protect any exported files, and contact us if you suspect unauthorised access.

9. Children

BarakahFlow is not intended for children under 13, or anyone below the minimum age required to use the service where they live. If you believe a child has provided personal information without the required permission, please contact us so we can review and address it.

10. Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will update the date above and provide any additional notice required by law.

For privacy questions or requests, email barakahflowapp@gmail.com.
